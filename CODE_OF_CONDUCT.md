# Code of Conduct

Spider TV Launcher is a friendly fan project. Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Be a good neighbour

- Be kind, patient and respectful.
- Give helpful feedback on the work, never attack the person.
- Help newcomers. Everyone starts somewhere.

## Not okay

- Harassment, insults, hate speech or discrimination of any kind.
- Spam, scams or advertising unrelated to the project.
- Sharing anyone's private information.
- Posting pirated content or leaked material.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and PRs, and may block anyone who breaks these rules. To report a problem, open an issue or contact the maintainer through GitHub ([@Fardin7798](https://github.com/Fardin7798)).

This code is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
